# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, and reviews.
- Assume good faith and give useful, specific feedback.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a suggestion; disagreement is fine, hostility is not.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory comments.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Using this project to promote illegal activity or to share resources whose primary purpose is unauthorized access to systems you do not own.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainer at **nadifakiyama@gmail.com**. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated, and will result in a response deemed appropriate to the circumstances. Maintainers may remove comments, commits, code, issues, and contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and may ban repeat offenders.
